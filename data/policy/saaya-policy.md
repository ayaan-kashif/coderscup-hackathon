# Saaya Relief Programme — Policy (FICTIONAL)

*Prototype policy for Coder's Cup '26. Not an official document. Clause IDs are cited by the rule set and the policy assistant.*

## Section 1

### SRP-POL-1.1 — Purpose

The Saaya Relief Programme (fictional) gives a quarterly cash transfer of Rs 12,000 to widows in financial need. This is a prototype policy written for a hackathon; it is not an official document.

### SRP-POL-1.2 — No fees

The programme never charges any fee for registration, processing or payment. Anyone asking for money to release a payment is committing fraud.

### SRP-POL-1.3 — Official messages

Official messages come only from the sender IDs SAAYA and 7240. Official messages never contain links and never ask for a PIN, a code or a photo of a CNIC.

## Section 2

### SRP-POL-2.1 — Who may apply

The applicant must be a woman whose identity is confirmed in the identity registry.

### SRP-POL-2.2 — Age

The applicant must be at least 18 years old on the date of the decision.

### SRP-POL-2.3 — Widowhood

The applicant's husband must be deceased, as shown by a death record in the civil registry or a signed death certificate.

### SRP-POL-2.4 — Time since death

The husband's death must have occurred within the ten years before the application.

### SRP-POL-2.5 — Remarriage

An applicant who has remarried after her husband's death is not eligible.

## Section 3

### SRP-POL-3.1 — Household income

The household income must not exceed Rs 40,000 per month.

### SRP-POL-3.2 — Residence

The applicant must live in a district served by the office where she applies.

## Section 4

### SRP-POL-4.1 — Identity card validity

An expired identity card is a warning for the officer and not a reason for rejection.

### SRP-POL-4.2 — Documents

A clear photo of the identity card and the death certificate is required. If a photo is unclear, the applicant is asked for one specific retake, at most twice.

## Section 5

### SRP-POL-5.1 — Decisions

Only an officer may reject an application. Every rejection states a reason code, and the applicant may complain.

### SRP-POL-5.2 — Payment

An approved payment is held for a short cooling-off period before it is sent, and the exact amount is confirmed to the applicant by voice.

### SRP-POL-5.3 — Payout changes

A change of payout wallet needs the applicant's PIN, an officer's approval, a cooling-off period and a notice to the old number.

## Section 6

### SRP-POL-6.1 — Helpers

A community helper may assist a family only while that family has unlocked its case. Helpers never collect money or PINs.

### SRP-POL-6.2 — Complaints

An applicant who received less than the full amount can file a complaint with one tap; the complaint is linked to the payment and to any helper.

## Section 7

### SRP-POL-7.1 — Consent

The applicant can see who holds her data, hear who opened it, and withdraw consent. Withdrawal pauses the case.

### SRP-POL-7.2 — Corrections

The applicant can ask for a wrong field to be corrected; an officer reviews the request and the old value is kept in history.
